# Slow Burn — App Store listing v2 (Guideline 1.1 remediation) — DRAFT, NOT SUBMITTED

> **Why this file exists.** Apple rejected under **Guideline 1.1 — Safety — Objectionable
> Content**, citing the app's **marketing / metadata** (description, promotional text,
> keywords). Apple warned that resubmitting "with similar marketing and concept" will
> draw the same or additional violations. So this is a genuine reframe of the store
> marketing, not a word swap.
>
> **What changed in principle:** every reference to a *sexual act, sexual anatomy, or a
> sex-therapy service* is gone from the metadata. What the app actually is — a staged,
> consent-gated Sensate Focus program — is unchanged and still stated honestly. The
> reframe is onto **connection, communication, and relationship wellness**.
>
> The clinical specifics (boundaries per stage, anatomy-neutral vs. clinical wording)
> still live **inside the app**, behind the first-launch acknowledgement, where 17+
> instructional content belongs. Apple's objection was to the *storefront*, not to that.
>
> **STATUS: DRAFT.** Nothing here has been pushed to App Store Connect. `store/store-listing.md`
> is left as-is until Jonathan signs off on this voice.

## Description

**BEFORE** — see `store/store-listing.md`. Offending passages listed in the table above.

**AFTER**
```
Slow Burn is a guided closeness program — a calm, structured way to rebuild connection at your own pace, on your own or with a partner.

It's built on Sensate Focus, a structured, step-by-step method developed by clinical researchers in the 1970s and used by licensed therapists ever since. Instead of a single session, Slow Burn turns the protocol into a six-stage program you move through one unhurried step at a time.

HOW IT WORKS
• A staged program — from simple, pressure-free connection through to full integration — with clear, clinical guidance for each session. Every stage has a solo form as well as a partnered one.
• A readiness gate that removes the awkward part. With a partner, the next stage opens only when you have BOTH privately tapped "I feel ready." No negotiating, no pressure.
• Private reflections after each session. You each answer a few questions on your own — and Slow Burn only ever surfaces what you BOTH named. Never the rest.
• Gentle, never-nagging reminders to keep the practice going.

FOR EVERY KIND OF PERSON
Slow Burn adapts its language to you — your pronouns, your words for your partner, your relationship structure. It assumes no gender, no orientation, and no particular shape of relationship.

WHO IT'S FOR
Anyone reconnecting after a long stretch of drift — a new baby, a health change, a recovery, a stressful season, or simply years of two people moving at different speeds. It's for feeling close again, calmly and privately.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account. No ads. No analytics. Nothing is collected about you. Your identity settings and your written notes never leave your device. If you pair two phones, they agree on a key only they hold and everything between them is end-to-end encrypted — the relay in the middle carries scrambled bytes it cannot read, and keeps none of them.

A NOTE FROM US
Slow Burn is a wellness and education program. It is not medical advice, and it is not a substitute for care from a qualified clinician. If you are managing pain, a medical condition, recovery, or a history of trauma, please work with a licensed therapist or a pelvic-floor physical therapist. The app links you to trusted professional directories to find one.
```

### Line-by-line diff of the description

| Was | Now | Why |
|---|---|---|
| "a guided **intimacy** program" | "a guided **closeness** program" | de-escalates the opening line |
| "rebuild **physical closeness**" | "rebuild **connection**" | drops the physical qualifier |
| "the touch-based method **sex therapists** have used since Masters & Johnson" | "a structured, step-by-step method developed by **clinical researchers** in the 1970s and used by **licensed therapists** ever since" | same claim, same truth (Masters & Johnson *were* clinical researchers; AASECT therapists *are* licensed) — no sexual-service term |
| "from unhurried, **non-genital** touch through to integration" | "from simple, **pressure-free connection** through to full integration" | removes the anatomical reference |
| "a **consent** gate" / "the next stage **unlocks**" | "a **readiness** gate" / "the next stage **opens**" | "consent gate" reads as sexual-consent framing to a reviewer skimming for 1.1; "opens" also avoids the earlier IAP confusion Apple flagged separately |
| "**desire differences**, postpartum recovery, menopause, **erectile changes**, recovery after illness" | "a new baby, a health change, a recovery, a stressful season, or years of two people moving at different speeds" | removes both sexual-function references; keeps every real audience |
| "**FROM THE TEAM**" | "**A NOTE FROM US**" | cosmetic |
| "a licensed **sex therapist** or pelvic-floor physical therapist" | "a licensed **therapist** or a pelvic-floor physical therapist" | same referral, no sexual-service term |

**The one judgment call:** the word **touch** survives, once, in "HOW IT WORKS" → it does
not, actually — it is gone from the description entirely in the AFTER copy above. Sensate
Focus *is* a touch protocol, so if you feel the listing has become vague about what the
user actually does, the safest honest re-add is "guided, unhurried touch" in HOW IT WORKS.
"Touch" on its own is not objectionable (massage and bodywork apps use it freely) — but
given Apple has now rejected this listing repeatedly, my recommendation is to ship this
round **without** it and add it back later once the app is approved and stable.

## How this gets pushed

**There is no fastlane metadata path in this repo.** There is no `fastlane/` directory, no
`metadata/en-US/*.txt`, and `scripts/asc-submit.sh` states explicitly (line 15) that it
"uploads no metadata". So today this listing must be **typed into App Store Connect by
hand**.

Two options, Jonathan's call:

1. **Manual (fastest, zero setup).** Paste each field above into ASC. Promotional text
   alone can be updated without a new review; name, subtitle, keywords, description and
   screenshots all ship with a version.
2. **Add a fastlane deliver lane (better long-term).** Create
   `fastlane/metadata/en-US/{name,subtitle,description,promotional_text,keywords}.txt`
   from the AFTER blocks above plus a `Deliverfile`, and add a `deliver` step to
   `codemagic.yaml` using the existing App Store Connect API key integration. That makes
   the listing reviewable in git and pushable programmatically — worth doing given how
   far ASC has already drifted from `store/store-listing.md` (the `: Couples Intimacy`
   name suffix is in ASC but has never been in this repo).
